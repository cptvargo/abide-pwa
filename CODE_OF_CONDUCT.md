# Code of Conduct for ABIDE PWA

## 🌿 Our Commitment

ABIDE is a Scripture-centered project created to support spiritual growth and deepen biblical understanding.  
We are committed to maintaining a respectful, encouraging, and collaborative environment for all trusted contributors.

This Code of Conduct outlines expectations for participation and the standards we uphold.

---

## 📘 Standards of Conduct

Contributors are expected to:

- Treat all team members with respect, patience, and humility
- Provide constructive feedback without hostility or personal attacks
- Maintain theological accuracy and integrity when working with Scripture
- Uphold high ethical standards in communication and collaboration
- Respect the proprietary nature of the project and its data

Examples of acceptable behavior:

- Offering thoughtful technical feedback
- Asking clarifying questions respectfully
- Supporting other contributors
- Acknowledging mistakes and correcting them quickly
- Providing documentation for your code changes

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are prohibited:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Hostile theological debates or divisive commentary
- Sharing proprietary project files or code outside the team
- Unauthorized redistribution of Scripture, translation, or insight data
- Disruptive or intentionally harmful changes to the project
- Misrepresentation of the ABIDE project or its author

---

## 🛡 Enforcement

Project maintainers (led by **Jesus Vargas**) are responsible for:

- Reviewing and resolving reported violations
- Taking corrective action when necessary
- Protecting the integrity and safety of the project

Violations may result in:

- A warning
- Temporary or permanent removal from collaboration
- Revocation of contribution privileges
- Further action if legal/licensing boundaries are violated

---

## 📩 Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience misconduct, report it privately to:

**Jesus Vargas**  
Owner & Maintainer  
ABIDE Bible App

## 🙏 Our Heart Behind This Project

ABIDE is more than a technical project — it is a discipleship tool crafted with care, prayer, and theological intention.

Every contributor plays a role in preserving:

- accuracy
- clarity
- spiritual integrity
- excellence

Thank you for honoring these commitments.
